Scrapjaw privacy policy

Koderium · Last updated 31 October 2026

Scrapjaw is a game made by Koderium. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Scrapjaw has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases. It doesn't send your gameplay, your device details or anything else about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (silver, unlocks, settings and quest progress) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device, so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves your device. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, deletes it.

Things the game loads from the internet

To run, the game loads its 3D library from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and its fonts from Google Fonts, and the game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, these services see the technical details of the request, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies: Cloudflare, Google and GitHub. After the first start, the game works offline from a copy saved on your device.

Permissions

The game may make your phone vibrate during play. You can turn this off in the game's settings. It asks for no other permissions.

Children

Scrapjaw doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
