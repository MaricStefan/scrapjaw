Scrap­jaw

Drive a tank that eats the battlefield. Swallow scrap and soldiers, bolt on guns, flatten four fortresses.

  • Drag anywhere on screen to steer. Weapons fire by themselves.
  • The main turret tracks on its own. Other guns only shoot from the side they're mounted on, so turn to aim.
  • Drive over anything small enough to eat. Beat a boss to grow and eat bigger things.
  • Hit Boost to ram hard or escape. Tap a module's icon to fire its special ability.

Install: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
