Drive a tank that eats the battlefield. Grow, upgrade, smash four bosses.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Roll out in a tiny tank and eat everything in your path.

Scrapjaw is a fast action roguelike for one thumb. Steer anywhere on the screen, and your guns fire on their own. Swallow scrap, soldiers and huts to grow, pick an upgrade at every level, and bolt new weapons onto your hull until your tank is a rolling fortress. Then take down the boss waiting at the end of each stage.

GROW BY EATING
Everything small enough goes down the hatch. Beat a boss and your tank grows big enough to swallow trucks, towers and tanks.

BUILD A DIFFERENT TANK EVERY RUN
• 19 modules for the front, sides, rear and top: autocannons, rocket pods, flamethrowers, mine droppers, drones, howitzers, homing missiles, smoke dischargers and more
• Every module has its own special ability and two mastery choices
• Pick upgrades as you level up, and grab artifacts from supply chests

REAL TANKS THAT EVOLVE
Start as a Scout, a Standard or a Bulwark. After every boss, choose speed or armor, and watch your tank grow into the next model of its family, from the T-34 to the T-54, or from the KV-1 to the Conqueror.

YOUR LOADOUT, YOUR STYLE
Before each run, pick your chassis, a captain with a perk and a drawback, a crew that rides on your deck and fights beside you, a starting module and a starting artifact.

THREE BATTLEFIELDS, TWELVE BOSSES
Fight through green farmland, a smoking iron foundry and burning ashlands. Each has its own enemies and four bosses, from a drilling war machine to an armored train and a burrowing wyrm.

ENDLESS OVERTIME
Cleared the map? Push on into Overtime, where night falls, mortars rain down and the silver keeps coming for as long as you survive.

• Daily quests and long-term milestones
• Plays offline
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• Made for phones in landscape
