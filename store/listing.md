# Google Play store listing: Scrapjaw

Paste these into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing. The limits in brackets are Play's; every text below fits them.

## Search (ASO) notes
Play has no keyword field: it ranks on the **app name**, the **short description** and the **full description**, plus installs, ratings and retention. So the words people search for have to appear in those texts, naturally. Play rejects keyword stuffing, lists of keywords, emoji or ALL CAPS in the name, and claims like "best", "#1", "free" or "top" in the name, icon or screenshots.

Keywords we target, most important first, and where each one appears:

| Keyword | Name | Short | Full |
|---|---|---|---|
| tank game / tank battle | ✓ (tank) | ✓ | ✓ |
| roguelike / roguelite | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| offline game / no wifi | | ✓ | ✓ |

## Full description (max 4000)

Roll out in a tiny tank and eat everything in your path.

Scrapjaw is a fast offline tank roguelike you play with one thumb. Steer anywhere on the screen and your guns fire on their own, like in your favorite survivor and bullet heaven games. Swallow scrap, soldiers and huts to grow, pick an upgrade at every level, and bolt new weapons onto your hull until your tank is a rolling fortress. Then take on the boss waiting at the end of each stage.

EAT AND GROW
Everything small enough goes down the hatch. Beat a boss and your tank grows big enough to swallow trucks, towers and other tanks.

BUILD A DIFFERENT TANK EVERY RUN
• 19 modules for the front, sides, rear and top: autocannons, rocket pods, flamethrowers, mine droppers, drones, a tesla coil, howitzers, homing missiles, troop and escort bays, smoke dischargers and more
• Every module has its own special ability and a mastery choice at level 8
• Pick upgrade cards as you level up, and open parachute supply drops for 28 different artifacts

HISTORIC TANKS THAT EVOLVE
Start as a Scout, a Standard or a Bulwark. After every boss, choose speed or armor and watch your tank turn into the next model of its family: from the T-34 to the T-54, from the AMX-13 to the M8 AGS, or from the KV-1 to the IS-3 and the Conqueror. Twenty-one tanks from WW2 to the end of the Cold War, all built in a chunky low-poly style.

TWELVE BOSS FIGHTS AGAINST REAL WAR MACHINES
Each region ends in giant historic vehicles with their own attacks: the KV-2, the Maus and the Karl-Gerät mortar, the Sherman Crab and the Sturmtiger, an armored train, twin T-35s and the Schwerer Gustav railway gun.

YOUR LOADOUT, YOUR STYLE
Before each run, pick your chassis, one of 16 captains with a perk and a drawback, a crew that rides on your deck and fights beside you (riflemen, a mortar team, a marksman and more), a starting module and a starting kit. Then paint your tank, add an emblem and a pennant, and bring a mascot.

NINE BATTLEFIELDS IN THREE REGIONS
Drive across a world map of green farmland, a smoking iron foundry and burning ashlands. Each region has its own enemies and hazards, from steam vents and shockwaves to lava pools and sweeping lasers, and three battlefields that get tougher and pay better.

ENDLESS OVERTIME
Cleared the map? Push on into Overtime, where night falls, mortars rain down and the silver keeps coming for as long as you survive.

ALSO IN SCRAPJAW
• Daily quests and long-term milestones that unlock new modules and crew
• Plays fully offline, no wifi needed
• No ads, no accounts, no data collected
• Short runs that fit a coffee break
• Made for phones in landscape, with haptics and a synthesized soundtrack
